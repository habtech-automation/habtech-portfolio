About

Hi, I'm Habeebullah, an AI Automation Specialist.

I specialize in building AI agents, chatbots, and automated workflows using n8n, APIs, and AI models like OpenAI and Claude. I'm drawn to the systems layer of a business: how a lead becomes a qualified contact, how a document turns into structured data, how a question gets answered without anyone typing a reply.

I work directly, one project at a time. I map the actual flow before building anything, then test it against real edge cases, not just the happy path. I enjoy the parts most people skip, error handling, validation, the small decisions that decide whether a workflow actually holds up once it's live.
